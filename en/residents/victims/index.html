    Names of Those Who Died in the Wang Fuk Court Fire

    繁體中文

    This page records 168 names grouped by the five manslaughter counts described in the HK01 report. The sequence follows the report, rather than the order of deaths. The charge groupings do not constitute findings of guilt.

    Source and transcription

    • Original report: HK01 report listing all 168 names, by 陳蓉 (in Chinese).
    • Publication date: 10 June 2026, 17:10; page updated on 2 September 2026, 16:00 (HKT).
    • Case number: WKCC 2702/2026.
    • Collection date and method: 26 November 2026 (HKT); the user supplied an extract, which was checked against HK01's Traditional Chinese page. This is a transcription of the reported list; the original charge sheet was not separately obtained.
    • Name display: Chinese names are displayed in Traditional Chinese, with script conversion applied to some source spellings. Consult the linked report for the original forms. Existing Traditional Chinese forms 余 and 峰, uncommon characters 𡖖, 鍚 and 琝, and Latin-script names retain their reported forms. The annotation 何偉豪（消防員） identifies a firefighter.
