# Wang Fuk Court Fire Linear Timeline

**Updated through: November 26, 2026 (Hong Kong time).** The historical table retains casualty figures announced at each point. November and December entries refer to 2025; January entries refer to 2026. Later developments appear in the follow-up section below.

**Event Overview**: A fire broke out at approximately 14:50 on November 26, 2025. The scaffolding blaze spread to the roof within 10 minutes and burned for 43 hours until extinguished on the 28th; 79 people were injured. Police subsequently completed identification on January 15, 2026, confirming 168 deaths and no missing persons unaccounted for.
